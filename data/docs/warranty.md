# Warranty

## Coverage
All electronics include a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase that covers manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, water damage, and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## Extended warranty
An extended warranty plan adds 2 additional years of coverage for $29.99. It must be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase.

## Making a claim
To file a warranty claim, submit the order number and a photo or video of the defect through the support portal. Approved claims receive a repair or replacement within 10 business days.
