# Shipping Policy

## Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. It is free on orders over $50 and otherwise costs $4.99. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

## Order tracking
A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of the order shipping. Orders placed after 2 PM Eastern Time ship on the next business day.
