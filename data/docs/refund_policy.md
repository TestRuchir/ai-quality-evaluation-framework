# Refund Policy

## Refund window
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30 days, purchases are eligible for store credit only, up to 60 days after delivery. No refunds or store credit are offered after 60 days.

## Eligibility
Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging with proof of purchase. Final-sale items, gift cards, and digital downloads are non-refundable.

## Refund processing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is inspected. Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.
